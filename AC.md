You are required to create a personal website to showcase your ePortfolio and career direction.

There is no compulsory website-building platform. You may use:

AI-assisted website-building tools introduced in Week 05
No-code website builders such as Canva, Wix, Adobe Portfolio, Google Sites, etc.
A more technically advanced approach if you already have web-development experience
We recommend using the AI-supported approach introduced in Week 05, as this provides an opportunity to develop useful digital and AI capabilities. However, the most important requirement is that your final website meets the ASM2 expectations.

Your ePortfolio should tell your professional story
Your website should be targeted towards your chosen career direction and designed with your prospective audience, such as employers and industry professionals, in mind.

A strong ePortfolio will normally include a purposeful selection of:

Home section with your name, professional headline and career direction
About section with a concise professional biography
Education, relevant courses and certifications
Work experience and internships
Leadership, volunteering and extracurricular activities
Selected projects and course artefacts
Skills and graduate capabilities supported by evidence
Achievements, awards or professional development
Appropriate contact details and professional links
The key expectation is quality rather than simply quantity. Do not only upload or list your projects. For important artefacts, provide enough information to help the audience understand what you did, your contribution, what you learned, the capabilities you developed, and how the experience supports your career direction.
